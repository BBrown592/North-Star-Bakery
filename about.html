Our Story

We started as a small mom-and-pop shop founded by my grandfather. He filled each bread and pastry with so much passion. We wanted to continue sharing his love-filled recipes with our community.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We handcraft and prepare all our products in-house. Fresh items are prepared each morning. Anything left after closing is thrown away to ensure freshness. We source our products from local organic farms and use high quality ingredients for our highly valued customers.
